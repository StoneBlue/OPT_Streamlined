OPT Spaceplane Continued

3.4.9.5

* Added J & K belt radiators.
* Added wrapper titanium radiators for H, J, K.
* Balance pass on wrapper radiators and solar panels.
* Gave wrapper intakes minimal drag cubes. They were horribly draggy due to their great surface area at non-zero AoA.
* Updated Dark Energy power spheres: They are now Sterling Systems style pebble bed reactors (fission type, thermals category).
  * Added generator bays (cloned from cargo bays) to hold them and provide generous thermoelectric conversion.
  * Added System Heat compatibility.
* Deprecated custom tech nodes.
* Hard deprecated old RCS parts.
* Added inline Mk2 and 2.5m RCS parts.
* Updated System Heat thermal nozzles config.
  * Affected engines produce 1/10 the previous amounts of waste heat.
  * Added/Moved LP-R Irradiance to this subcategory.
* Fixed Kerbal Actuators config pointing to OPT Legacy for the alternate models for the Slipstream engine (RCS) and Skylight tilt engine. Affected engines became invalid and would not appear.


3.4.9.2

* Added texture options for Procedural Parts.
* Added supersonic ram air turbine.
* Added large radial intake.
* Added wrapper intakes for J, K bodies.
* Added wrapper solar panels and radiators for H, J, K bodies.
* Fixed (killed) 1km spawn kraken in Avatar cargo ramp, Humpback cargo ramp, J Deployment Bay, but not the Stail cargo bay(s).
* Fixed (killed) 1km spawn kraken in J Large Docking Port but it seems to no longer actually dock.
* Fixed collider issue in huge wings.
* Moved the following parts (series) from Legacy to Main:
  * Wings2 (series D to G).
  * Power Spheres.
  * All 2.5m.
  * All VTOL engines except Humpback wrappers.
* Moved all patches and sounds to Reconfig.
* Removed radiator module from fuselages and wings.
* Reorganized parts.
* Updated all crewed parts to have stockalike blue window glass.
* Updated dry masses on all parts that hold fuel. They should meet the CryoTanks standard.
* Updated fuel switch options in Power Spheres. Some additions and subtractions have occurred.
* Updated engines: 
  * WarpJets are now single-mode Pulsed Inductive Thruster (still thrusty like we like it).
  * Bubble is now fully electric. Usability might be reduced.
  * ARI-73 (NTR), LP-R Irradiance (NTR) and Helicarrier are now thermal nozzles.
  * LP-R Prominence (LFO) are no longer "SCOOP" hybrid plasma voodoo.
  * J-61 is now a ramjet + scramjet. Operates between Mach 1.2 & 15.
* Updated localization on parts that have cargo doors, ramps, docking ports and ladders.
